Welcome to SlozOS Pro

Sit back while SlozOS Pro installs. It only takes a few minutes, and everything you need for work and play will be ready when it's done.

Familiar from the first click

The Start button and your apps sit in the middle of the taskbar. Drag a window to the top of the screen to snap it into a layout, just like you're used to.

Ready for work

LibreOffice opens and saves Word, Excel and PowerPoint files. Remote Desktop, email, calendar and a fast, private web browser are all included.

Light and quick

SlozOS Pro starts fast and stays smooth, even on older or 4 GB PCs. Nothing runs in the background that you didn't ask for.

Safe and up to date

Updates arrive automatically from the Software app, with no viruses to worry about. Your files stay on your PC.
